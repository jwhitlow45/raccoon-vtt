## 1. Summary

- **Three groups.** The DM's list of panels, on a phone and in the side panel's More, reads In play (Token, Initiative, Dice, Log, Journal, Areas, and Party on a phone), Run the table (DM, Scenes, Ambiance), Prepare (Monsters, Encounters, Characters, Outliner, Library), then a line and Menu. The side panel's four tabs (Token, Initiative, Dice, Log) do not change.
- **One Ambiance panel** takes the place of Time and Sound. It holds two folding sections, Time and Sound, each with a one-line summary while folded, and between them a Weather row for the scene the DM is looking at that opens that scene's weather in Scenes (scenes-reorg 4.2). It starts compact: Time folded once a clock runs, Sound open (the owner's choice). Sound's first page puts Effects right under Now playing (the owner's choice). A scene's weather and its own sound are the scene's settings, in Scenes (scenes-reorg 4 and 5).
- **Players** get Ambiance while they would have had Time or Sound, with those two sections and no folds. Nothing new reaches them.
- **Folds that remember**, per device, in Ambiance, the DM panel, Scenes (New scene and scenes-reorg 3's six groups) and This device. Outside Ambiance, what is used in play starts open, and setup starts folded on a phone and open from 900 px up. Anything sent to a folded section (a pasted image, a waiting upload, the chip) opens it.
- **Organization only.** No op, no projection, no setting, no new control. Old panel ids still open the right panel.

- Rejected: a player Weather line (new feature), folds for players (taps for two short sections), listing Ambiance for every player always (an empty panel for a room with no clock or sound), Time's "isn't sharing" line alone in the empty state (it says nothing of sound), closing a restored empty Ambiance (focus and the split would move under the player).
