# raccoon-vtt: join requests the DM approves

Status: built, 2026-11-02. The design passed eight review rounds (Review, at the end, says what they changed). Draft 5 settled two choices: an admitted ask gets a short-lived token, so nothing has to carry an approval to a device later, and a new player's member is written before its grant, with ask tokens in a table of their own. Code references are from the build's base, after v0.3.1, where OP_VERSION is 41 and SNAPSHOT_VERSION 31. The feature steps neither. As built, at the end, lists every place the build went its own way.

The planning list ([mvp.md](mvp.md) section 1, Later) says: "Join requests that the DM approves." The project owner set the Later order on 2026-11-02 (D-261102-007), with this second, among the easy items. So the design stays small: no room state, no ops, no new socket kind.

## Scope

- **In**: a room setting for how new devices join, a way for a newcomer without the password to ask, a live answer from any DM device (Let in or Turn away), and the limits, lifetimes and logging that keep a public room ID from becoming a spam or snooping channel. The member-before-grant order also changes today's `join/complete` for a new player.
- **Out**: accounts, a lobby with chat, asking for DM rights (the DM link and DM invites stay the only way), table displays (still paired by code), a cooldown after a kick, and any change for a device that already holds a grant.
- **Fixed**: authority still comes only from the grant (design 10.2). A request never writes a grant by itself. Only a DM's tap does, in one transaction. Nothing about the room reaches a requester before that tap. A requester holds no session and buys no allowance until then.

## 1. Joining today, in short

A new device sends the player password to `POST /api/rooms/:id/join` (server/http/join.ts), which checks the per-address limits, the join challenge and the argon2 hash, then mints a 5-minute ticket. `join/complete` spends the ticket to continue as a listed player (never a former DM) or to become a new one, and writes the `player` grant at `player_epoch` under the room row lock, then adds a new member through the room actor. The player link carries the password in its fragment. DMs come in by the DM link or a one-time invite. Kicking deletes grants, but a kicked player who knows the password can rejoin, so keeping someone out means changing the password ([FEATURES.md](../../FEATURES.md), Rooms and joining). Owner decision 9 ([design.md](design.md) 17) rules out anything that lets a stranger who knows a room ID lock its new devices out.

## 2. What people see and do

### 2.1 The setting (Members, DM only)

Menu, Members gains a section **Joining** above Player password, a `RadioCards` group with the legend "How new players join". It shows the shared delayed "Loading…" until its first fetch answers, saves on tap, shows busy while saving, and says "Saved." or the error in its status line. Every DM device updates at once. Picking the mode already set changes nothing.

| Value (`rooms.join_mode`) | Label | Help |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `password` (every room starts here) | With the password | The password and the player link let new devices in. |
| `ask` | With the password, or by asking | Someone without the password can ask, and a DM lets them in. |
| `ask_only` | Only by asking | Every new device asks, and a DM lets it in. The password and the player link stop letting anyone new in. Devices already in stay in, and a kicked player stays out. If asking stops working, switch to With the password, or by asking, and share the player link. |

Switching to With the password ends every request still waiting. Switching between the two asking modes keeps them as they are, since nothing in a request depends on which of the two it was made in. Changing the player password keeps them too: the DM still decides each one, and a let-in writes its grant at the new epoch. Entering Only by asking drops every outstanding join ticket, as a password change does. Leaving Only by asking when the room somehow holds no password to go back to is refused: "This room has no player password to go back to. Set a new player password first." (`no_password`).

In Only by asking, the Player link section (client/features/dm/Access.tsx) adds one line under its buttons: "Only by asking is on. This link opens the join page, where new players ask to join." The link itself is unchanged, and "Copy player link" still copies it.

### 2.2 A newcomer asks (the join page)

The join page (client/pages/Join.tsx) learns the mode from `GET /api/rooms/:id`, which already answers anyone with the room's name. Before it shows any card it asks `GET .../join-requests/mine` (5.2), with the shared delayed "Loading…": a device with a live request goes straight to the waiting card, in any tab or after a reload. A device the DM already let in never reaches the join page, since its cookie names the session that holds the grant and the room route's `GET /me` opens the room. The room route now opens the room only when `GET /me` returns a member, and shows the join page otherwise.

- **With the password**: as today. No ask is offered.
- **With the password, or by asking**: the "Join the game" card stays first. Under it a card titled "No password?" says "Ask the DM to let you in." with an **Ask to join** button, which swaps both cards for the ask card. A player link still joins at once.
- **Only by asking**: the ask card shows in the password card's place. A player link opened here sends nothing, and the card's notice reads "This room lets new players in when a DM says yes, so ask here." The fragment is still cleared before render, as today.

A refusal that shows the mode changed (`asks_off`, `asks_only`) makes the page read `GET /api/rooms/:id` again and show the cards for the new mode.

The **ask card** ("Ask to join"): the help line "The DM sees your name and color, then lets you in or says no.", then **Your name** (the remembered name filled in, 16 px field) and **Your color** (the ten member colors with no "taken" marks, since the requester may learn nothing about the room, starting on the first one that is not the DM's yellow, `freeColor([], [MEMBER_COLORS[0]])`), the Turnstile widget when the room asks for it (also when an ask comes back `turnstile_required`, after which it sends again once solved, as the join challenge does), and **Ask to join** (busy "Asking…", refuses a second tap). In the With the password, or by asking mode a **Back** button returns to the password card. Each ask runs inside `navigator.locks.request("rvtt-ask")` where the browser has it, so two tabs of one browser never ask at once and the second always carries the cookie the first was given.

The **waiting card** ("Waiting for the DM"): "You asked to join as Quinn.", the shared loading line "Waiting for the DM…", the help "Keep this page open. A request ends after 10 minutes.", and **Cancel**. While it shows, the page holds a screen wake lock where the browser offers one (client/lib/wakeLock.ts, as the room does). A lost connection adds the muted line "Reconnecting…" and keeps waiting. A reload, a new tab, a locked phone or a phone in a pocket keeps the request for its whole 10 minutes. Cancel while a DM's let-in is committing shows "The DM is letting you in…" and keeps waiting for the answer. Every answer names its request, and the page ignores an answer for a request it no longer waits on, so a late or reversed answer never moves it.

The outcomes, each a card whose heading takes focus:

| Outcome | Card | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Let in | none: the room opens, as after `join/complete` | |
| Turned away | "The DM said no" / "You can ask again in 15 minutes." | Back |
| No answer in 10 minutes | "No answer yet" / "The DM may be away. Ask again later, or ask the DM for the player link." | Ask again, Back |
| The DM chose With the password | "Requests are off" / "The DM stopped taking requests." The password card shows below. | Ask again, where requests are on again by the time the page shows it |
| Another tab took over | "Waiting in another tab" / "This request is waiting in another tab or window." This page stops asking the server. | Wait here |
| Ended otherwise (the server restarted before anyone answered) | "Request ended" / "That request ended. Ask again." | Ask again |

Before showing "Request ended", and on `already_in`, the page asks `GET /api/rooms/:id/me`. A current grant with its member opens the room instead, so an approved phone never reads "Request ended". The password path's "Who are you?" step does the same when `join/complete` answers `busy`: it asks `GET /me` and enters if the join went through, else tries again after 1, 2 and 5 s while the server settles the attempt (5.2), then says "Something went wrong. Try again."

Refusals of the ask itself show under its button: "Several people are waiting for the DM already. Try again in a few minutes." (`asks_full`, with no Retry-After), "The DM said no a few minutes ago. Ask again in 12 minutes." (`asked_recently`, from Retry-After, which counts only this device's own hold), "Too many requests from here. Try again in 40 minutes." (`rate_limited`, from this address's own window), "The server is busy. Try again in a moment." (`busy`), and the Turnstile words the join challenge already uses (client/pages/joinChallenge.ts).

### 2.3 The DM answers

- **Toast** on every DM device: "Quinn asks to join." with **Review**, which opens the DM panel. It shows for 12 seconds, as every toast with a button (`ACTION_TOAST_MS`), and the badge and the section stay after it goes. At most one join toast every 20 seconds: a later one names the newest and the count, "Quinn and 2 more ask to join." A DM device that opens the room with requests already waiting says "Quinn is waiting to join." once. A device toasts each request once, by id, across reconnects. Once the room passes 20 asks in an hour (4.3), asks stop raising toasts, and one toast says "Many people are asking to join. Turn them away, or switch Joining to With the password, or by asking." with **Review**.
- **Badge** on the DM panel's tab, and on a phone the Panels button: the waiting count, added to the PDF upload's (client/pages/Room.tsx `badges.dm`, `badgeLabels.dm`). The DM panel's count speaks "2 join requests", or "1 upload waiting, 2 join requests" with both. The Panels button speaks "2 unread, 1 upload waiting, 2 join requests" in that order, leaving out the parts at zero.
- **Join requests section** at the top of the DM panel while any request waits, gone when none does. Each request is a `Row`: the requested color's dot, the name, and "Asked just now" or "Asked 3 min ago". Under it **Let in** and **Turn away**, side by side where they fit and stacked where they do not. When the room has players a DM could carry on as, a `Select` labeled "Let in as" sits above the buttons, with "Someone new" (always the default) and each player who was never a DM. With two or more waiting, **Turn away all** ends the section. It turns away only the requests on screen when tapped.
- **Carrying on as a player**: picking a player in "Let in as" always shows the note "This device will be Quinn: Quinn's characters, tokens and whispers. Only pick Quinn if you know it is them.", with "Quinn is online now." added while that member is online. A typed name that matches a player's, compared after NFKC and case folding, only adds "The name they typed matches Quinn." as a hint, since look-alike letters defeat any match.
- **Busy and errors**, per row: "Letting in…" or "Turning away…" disable that row, and "Still letting Quinn in…" replaces the first when the server answers that it is still finishing (`202`), until the signal settles the row. Refusals read "Quinn is no longer asking.", "Another DM already answered.", "Quinn is in the room already.", "The room is full: it holds 50 members. Remove a member first.", "That player was just removed. Let them in as someone new.", "The server is busy. Try again in a moment.", or "Something went wrong. Try again." The list fetches again after any of them.
- **Fetching**: each fetch is numbered, and only the newest answer shows, as the player link's fetches are (client/features/dm/playerLinkLoader.ts). A failed fetch tries again after 1, 2, 5, 10 and then every 30 seconds, until one answers or a newer signal starts a fresh fetch. A section already showing keeps its rows with the muted line "Could not refresh. Trying again…".
- **Co-DMs**: every DM device gets the toast, the badge and the section. The first answer wins. While one device's let-in runs, the request's row shows busy on every DM device, its buttons off, and leaves every list once let in, or waits again if the let-in fails. It counts for no badge, toast or Turn away all meanwhile.
- **Ignoring is safe**: an unanswered request ends by itself after 10 minutes, and the requester cannot tell whether anyone saw it.
- **View as a player**: the section and new toasts wait until the DM leaves the view, as the Members page hides its DM tools there. The badge stays.

A let-in as someone new adds the member through the existing `sys.memberJoin`, so every device's log says "Quinn joins the room." as for any join. A let-in whose grant is known to have failed before it could commit takes that member back off, which logs "Quinn was removed from the room." as any removal does. A let-in as an existing player adds nothing to the log, as "Continue as" does today.

### 2.4 Phones, tablets and computers

- **Phone held upright**: the join page is the usual single column of cards. The toast shows where toasts show. Review opens the DM panel as a bottom sheet with the section at its top, its buttons stacked below each name at 320 px and at 130%.
- **Phone held sideways**: the join page scrolls in the same column. The DM panel opens as the right-hand sheet.

## 3. Rules and other tables

The SRD 5.2.1 has no rule about who sits at the table, so no rules text applies. Ideas from elsewhere, never code: Foundry VTT has no knock at all (the GM makes each player's user), so the closest models are video calls. Google Meet's "Ask to join" shows the host a named request with Admit and Deny and holds off a guest who keeps knocking after a denial. Zoom's waiting room adds Admit all. Here the DM gets the named knock and a mass Turn away, but no Let in all, since one careless tap must never let a crowd in.

## 4. Security

### 4.1 Threat model

Room IDs are not secrets (design 10.1): they sit in every link and on screen. So the attacker is anyone on the internet who knows one.

| Threat | Mitigation | What is left |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spam: many asks to annoy the DM | Per-address caps (4.3), Turnstile on every ask once the room passes 20 asks in an hour, rechecked where admissions are serialized, toasts quieted past that, one request per device per room, Turn away all, switching to With the password ends them all | Without a Turnstile secret, many addresses can keep asking |
| Filling the room's waiting places so real players cannot ask (Only by asking) | No hourly refusal per room (decision 9). The one per-room bound is 50 waiting places, a memory cap, under 8 per address. Turnstile past the threshold, Turn away all. The help text and FEATURES.md name the way out: switch to With the password, or by asking, and share the player link | About seven IPv6 /64s fill a room's 50 places. Devices already in are never affected |
| Turnstile outage in Only by asking, past the threshold | Asks fail closed with `503 turnstile_unavailable`, as every check does | The same way out: switch to With the password, or by asking |
| Impersonation: a stranger asks as "Quinn" to be let in as the real Quinn and read Quinn's whispers | "Someone new" is always the default. Every pick of an existing player shows what it hands over and whether that player is online. A matching name is only a hint. Former DMs are never offered or accepted | A DM who picks the player on purpose for a stranger. The same trust the password already carries (design 17, decision 3) |
| Snooping before approval | The requester gets only its own request's state (4.2). No roster, colors in use, member count, presence or who answered. `asks_full` and `rate_limited` carry no Retry-After from other people's asks | The room's name and join mode, which `GET /api/rooms/:id` already shows to anyone with the ID. From `asks_full`, that the room's places are taken. From a Turnstile request, that the room passed 20 asks this hour or that this address was turned away |
| Learning when the group plays (DM presence) | Asks are taken whether or not a DM is online. No answer and an absent DM look the same | A turn away shows a DM was there. A DM who wants no trace ignores the ask |
| Rows minted by asks | Only an admitted ask from a device with no session mints an ask token, inside the admission lock, in `ask_sessions`, never in `sessions`: no user agent, an hour's life | At most 60 an hour per address (its ask limit), each expired within the hour and deleted by the hourly cleanup |
| Request allowance amplification, now and after a rollback | The request limiter reads only `sessions`, so an ask token is no session to it on any route, counted by address like no cookie. An older build does not know `ask_sessions` either, so after a rollback an ask token is an unknown cookie there too | None beyond today's |
| Hijacking someone else's approval | A request is bound to the asking device's session or ask token (the HttpOnly room cookie) and a 256-bit id. Every check of both comes before a held wait is touched. Answer routes need a DM grant and the request's own room | None known |
| Losing an approval: a closed tab, a second Ask, asking another room, a password join, a DM link or a new room meanwhile | Every path that makes a session adopts an ask token the request carries (same id and token, under the ask row's lock), so pending requests keep following one identity, and the let-in writes the grant onto the session the device's cookie names. The room route's `GET /me` opens the room from any tab, and a second ask answers `already_in`. A promoted token's hash leaves the unknown-token cache as its promotion commits. Asks in one browser run one at a time under `navigator.locks` | A browser without Web Locks that asks two rooms in the same moment with no cookie keeps only the second token set. The first request cannot be waited on, its page offers Ask again, and a DM can still let it in, into a member nobody plays, which the DM removes |
| Dodging the turn-away hold with a fresh, invented or other-room token | The hold keys on the session or token and the address. While a room holds an address, an ask from it needs Turnstile unless it comes with a real session created before the hold | Without a Turnstile secret, a device that clears its cookie can ask again from the held address, within its 30 an hour |
| A ticket minted before or during the switch to Only by asking | `join/complete` reads `join_mode` in its `for update` select and refuses `asks_only`. The switch also drops the room's tickets | None |
| Rollback to a build without this feature | In Only by asking the real hash moves to `player_pw_hash_held` and `player_pw_hash` holds a sentinel that every older verify reads as wrong (5.4). Hashes move in one statement, only on real transitions | An older build lets nobody new in by password until its DM changes the password there |
| Races (two DMs, an answer against a cancel, a mode switch or delete during a let-in, a password join meanwhile, an ask replacing a request being decided, two rooms promoting one token) | One synchronous step per request, final admission checked in the same store, endings recorded during `deciding` and applied on failure, a per-room generation, the grant written under the room row lock, promotion under a lock on the ask row, `already_in` when the session holds any grant here with its member, and a "got in" mark from `join/complete` and `becomeDm` that outlives every admission that started before it (5.1) | None known |
| A grant without its member (a crash between the two, a failed grant), or an undo removing a member someone else claimed | Every path that grants a new player writes the member first and the grant second (5.2). A member in flight is kept out of "continue as" and the DM's carry-on list, so nobody else can claim it, Only a rollback known to precede the commit removes it, revoking by member, removing and revoking again as Remove does, and an unknown outcome keeps it. A crash in between leaves an unused offline member, never a grant without one. The room route shows the join page when `GET /me` has no member | A member nobody plays, which the DM removes |
| A stalled database or room actor | Bounded checkouts, statement and lock timeouts, a 5 s bound on each actor call with its unknown outcome kept and followed, and `CommitOutcomeUnknown` reconciled under the room lock against the exact grant (5.2) | A let-in stays `deciding` until its outcome is known |
| Two tabs on one request | A newer wait answers the older with `superseded`, and that page stops | None |
| Guessing the password through the ask | An ask never touches the password. In Only by asking, `join` refuses before the limits and the hash, and counts nothing | None |
| Words as an attack (a link, a fake "DM:" prefix, a very long name) | The existing `Name` schema (80 characters, trimmed), shown as plain text, never as a link or Markdown | A rude name on the DM's screen until Turn away |
| Cross-site asks from another page | Every route that changes state is a POST or DELETE under the CSRF rule (design 10.7). The new GETs only read | None |
| Memory growth and pool use | 500 live requests on the server, 50 a room, a sweep every 15 s, records kept 2 minutes after they end, one held wait per request. Admission waits on in-memory locks, never on a pooled connection, and its statements time out at 2 s | None |

### 4.2 What each device may receive

| Device | Receives |
| --- | --- |
| Requester, before a let-in | The room's name and join mode (already public), and its own request's state: waiting, in, turned away, expired, closed, superseded or gone. Never the roster, colors in use, the DMs, who is online, how many wait (only that the room's places are taken, from `asks_full`), or who answered |
| Requester, after a let-in | Exactly what any new player gets, through the usual snapshot |

### 4.3 Limits and lifetimes

- **Asks per address**, counted in `rate_events` so a restart resets nothing, by the address's limits form (`ipKey`, an IPv6 /64 as one): 30 an hour per room and 60 an hour across rooms. A let-in records `askin:<room>:<ip>`, and the per-room check counts asks less let-ins, so a table that gets in never runs short. Over a limit: `429 rate_limited`, with Retry-After from that address's own window.
- **Requests per minute**: an ask token is no session to the request limiter (design 10.3), which counts everything it sends at 120 a minute per address. A device that already had a real session asks with it and keeps that session's limits.
- **The room's challenge, never a refusal**: 20 asks in a sliding hour from all addresses make every ask in that room need a solved Turnstile check, when the server has a secret, and quiet the DM's toasts. It lifts an hour after the asks stop, as the join challenge does (`joinChallenged`, ratelimit.ts). It is rechecked where admissions are serialized, so a burst never slips its 21st ask past it.
- **Waiting at once**: 8 per address per room, 50 per room, 500 on the server, all memory bounds. Over that: `429 asks_full`, with no Retry-After.
- **One request per device per room**: a new ask from the same session or token replaces a waiting request, which ends as gone, and gets `409 request_deciding` while one is being decided.
- **After a turn away**: that session or token may not ask that room again for 15 minutes (`askno:<room>:<id>`, mirrored in memory, `429 asked_recently`). The hold also records the address and its start (`asknoip:<room>:<ip>`): during those 15 minutes, an ask in that room from that address needs a Turnstile check, where the server has a secret, unless it comes with a real session created before the hold began. So a fresh, invented or other-room token costs a check and spends the address's 30, while the other phones at the table can still ask once they pass the check.
- **Life**: 10 minutes from the ask, whatever the page does, as invites and display codes. An ask token expires an hour after its latest ask and the hourly cleanup deletes it. A wait holds for 25 seconds and then answers "waiting". A reverse proxy's response (read) timeout must be longer than that: Cloudflare's is 125 s in its current documentation, and [self-hosting.md](../self-hosting.md) says so for other proxies.
- **Admission deadline**: an ask has 30 seconds from its first step to its final admission, Turnstile and lock waits included. Past that, final admission answers `503 busy`.
- **Answers**: a let-in still counts as a successful join for the room and the requester's address (`recordSuccess`), and as a let-in for its address's ask count, but neither refuses it, since the DM is the gate. `join/complete` records nothing new, since `/join` already counted its success.

### 4.4 What is logged

- **Room log**: "Quinn joins the room." for a let-in as someone new, from the existing join event, and "Quinn was removed from the room." if its grant is known to have failed before it could commit. Nothing for an ask, a refusal, an expiry, a cancel or a carry-on.
- **Server log** (pino, info): "join request asked" with the room id and request id, "join request answered" with the outcome, the answering DM's member id and new or existing, "join requests challenged" when a room passes its threshold, "join outcome unknown" (warn) while a let-in's or a join's outcome is followed up, and "join mode changed" with the mode and member id. Endings without an answer at debug. Never the name, color, token, session, address or user agent. Fastify's request lines stay as they are.
- **Database**: `rooms.join_mode`, `rooms.player_pw_hash_held`, `ask_sessions` rows, and the `rate_events` rows above (pruned after a day as every row there).

## 5. Model and versions

### 5.1 Data and the request's life

- `rooms.join_mode text not null default 'password'`, checked to `password`, `ask` or `ask_only`, and `rooms.player_pw_hash_held text` (null outside Only by asking), each added by an idempotent `alter table ... add column if not exists`, and a new table `ask_sessions (id uuid primary key, token_hash bytea not null unique, created_at, expires_at)`, all in db/schema.sql. The columns are access data beside `player_pw_hash`, not game state.
- **The asking device**: a device with a live room session asks with it, as any route reads it (`lookup`). A device with none gets an ask token at admission (5.2): a row in `ask_sessions`, an hour's life, in the room cookie the app already uses, set only once admission succeeds. A later ask with the token moves its expiry to an hour from then, an update that touches only a row still in `ask_sessions`. The new `AskSessions` store (server/auth/askSessions.ts) reads the cookie for the ask, `mine`, wait and cancel routes, and `SessionStore.create` reads it to adopt a token (below). Nothing else, the request limiter included, ever reads `ask_sessions`.
- **Promotion**: one helper, `AskSessions.promote(tx, id)`, holds the ask row with `select ... for update`, inserts a `sessions` row with the same id and token hash, a null user agent and the normal 180 days, and deletes the ask row. It has two callers, each naming whose identity it promotes. The let-in passes the requester's id from the request record, never anything from its own request, which is the DM's, so the DM's session is untouched. `SessionStore.create` passes the caller's own cookie token, and only in the room store (`purpose` room, never the display store), so `ensure` (a password join, room creation, room import) and `becomeDm`'s create branch adopt the device's ask token instead of minting. `create` finds its target with the same one-statement, one-snapshot read of both tables, `purpose = 'room'` included, then locks the ask row it found. When the ask row is gone because a let-in just promoted it, `create` returns that `sessions` row with the cookie's token rather than minting. So the device's cookie names a real session with no cookie change, and every pending request, in any room, keeps following the same id. `becomeDm` then rotates the token on that id, as a DM claim always does. A let-in in another room that finds the ask row gone uses the `sessions` row with that id, and one that finds neither ends as gone. A failed transaction rolls the promotion back with it, and nothing is ever demoted. `#find` returns `user_agent is null` as its own flag, and `session()` fills the user agent from the session's own next request whenever that flag is set, apart from its slide and touch writes, never from the DM's request.
- **The unknown-token cache**: `SessionStore`'s 60-second cache of tokens that named no session is keyed by `sha256(token)`, which `#find` already computes, and its comment's invariant becomes: a token turns valid later only through promotion, which clears its hash here. `withTx` gains a list of after-commit callbacks, run synchronously as `commit` resolves, and a promotion registers two: delete the hash from the cache, and stamp the hash as promoted now. Both are idempotent, so they also run whenever the promotion may have committed: on `CommitOutcomeUnknown`, before the caller does anything else, and again in the reconciliation before any settlement is published, whatever it finds, a kicked grant included. So they happen before anything the caller does next (the wait's answer, the signal, the route's reply), and every request authenticated after the commit reads the database. Each lookup notes when it started, and a miss is not cached for a hash stamped after that start, so a lookup already in flight across the commit cannot put it back. A request whose own lookup already returned that miss is handled where it matters, at the ask (5.2). `AskSessions` keeps no negative cache.
- **Member before grant**: every path that grants a new player, the let-in and `join/complete` alike, goes through one helper, `admitPlayer` (new server/auth/admit.ts): the member first, through the room actor, then the grant in its transaction, then a check that the member is still there. While the attempt runs, its member is in flight: `continuable` and the DM list's `players` leave it out, so no other device or let-in can claim it. 5.2 gives the bounds and outcomes. A player grant without its member can then come only from data written before this change, which the room route treats as not in and the next grant write on that session replaces. `becomeDm`'s new-DM path keeps today's grant-first order, outside this feature: its secret is spent under the room lock in the grant's own transaction, so writing the member first would leave a DM member, offered by every later "This is me", for a claim whose secret then failed. Its rare crash gap now meets the join page and its "Were you the DM?" entry instead of a broken room.
- **The store**: `JoinRequests` in new server/auth/joinRequests.ts, beside tickets.ts, with an injected clock: `{id, roomId, generation, sessionId, ipKey, name, color, askedAt, state, endAfter?, memberId?, pending?, endedAt?}`, where `sessionId` is the session or ask token's id, plus places reserved by asks still checking, a generation counter per room, the in-memory admission locks, the turn-away holds, the "got in" marks that `join/complete` and `becomeDm` leave on a session and room for 60 seconds, twice the admission deadline, so every ask that started before a mark reaches final admission or its deadline while the mark stands, the members in flight per room, and the member removals still owed. A restart drops it. A sweep every 15 s on the injected clock, `unref`'d and cleared on close, ends what has expired, follows up unknown outcomes and owed removals (5.2), drops records ended over 2 minutes ago, and signals the DMs of every room it changed.
- **States**: `waiting` and `deciding` are live. `in`, `turned_away`, `expired`, `cancelled`, `closed` and `gone` are endings. `superseded` is an answer to a displaced wait, never a request's state. Every step is synchronous, so of two racing calls exactly one moves a request.
  - `waiting` to `deciding` when a DM's let-in takes it, to `turned_away` on a turn away (which sets the hold in memory in the same step), `cancelled` on Cancel, `closed` when the room switches to With the password, `gone` when a newer ask from the device replaces it, its room is deleted, or the device gets in another way (`join/complete` or `becomeDm`, which read the caller's cookie first and end the request of the session or token it names), and `expired` at 10 minutes. Switching between `ask` and `ask_only` changes nothing.
  - **Generation**: a room's counter is bumped only by what ends its requests: a switch to With the password, after it commits, and a delete, once when `manager.onDelete` runs and again after `gateway.deleteRoom` removes the row. An ask reads the generation before the mode and creates its request only if it is unchanged at final admission, else answers `asks_off` or `room_not_found`. A request remembers the generation it was made at.
  - **Deciding**: nothing but its own let-in moves a deciding request. An ending that arrives meanwhile (expiry, With the password, a delete, getting in another way, Cancel) is recorded in `endAfter`, and Cancel still answers `409 request_deciding`, since it cannot stop a grant already committing. Admission never replaces or ends it (5.2). A request leaves `deciding` only once its outcome is known: on success, `in` wins and `endAfter` is dropped. On a rollback known to precede the commit, it first takes back a member it added, then settles by the table below. A reconcile that cannot confirm the grant ends it as gone, with the member kept. While an actor call or a commit is still unknown, it stays `deciding`, past its 10 minutes if need be.

| A failed let-in, when | Settles as |
| --- | --- |
| `endAfter` holds an ending | that ending |
| the generation changed and the room is gone | gone |
| the generation changed and the room is With the password | closed |
| its 10 minutes are up | expired |
| `already_in`, or the session and token are both gone | gone |
| the commit's outcome stays unknown after the reconcile (the member stays) | gone |
| none of these (a full room, a removed carry-on member, a refused or timed-out step that wrote no grant) | waiting, its place kept |

- An ended record stays 2 minutes so a late wait learns its outcome. `in`, `turned_away`, `expired` and `closed` answer from it. `cancelled` and `gone` answer `404 request_gone`.

Every key and record uses `roomParam`'s canonical room id.

**The ask, in order.** (1) Read the room's generation, then its mode: `404 room_not_found`, or `403 asks_off` in With the password. (2) Read the device's session, else its ask token, without minting. A grant here with its member answers `409 already_in`, its request here being decided `409 request_deciding`, and its hold `429 asked_recently`. (3) Read the address's hourly counts (`rate_limited`), the waiting places (`asks_full`), and whether Turnstile is needed: the room is challenged, or its address is held and the ask has no real session from before the hold. (4) Reserve a place in memory, synchronously, so racing asks cannot overfill it while the next steps wait. (5) Verify Turnstile when step 3 says so, outside every lock. (6) Take the in-memory admission locks for the address and then for the room, always in that order. An ask past its 30 s deadline once it holds them answers `503 busy` with nothing recorded. Under them runs one short transaction, with a 2 s checkout and `set local statement_timeout = '2s'`: one select rechecks the address's counts and the room's challenge count, and an ask that now needs Turnstile but carried no verified token answers `403 turnstile_required`. Then one insert records `ask:<room>:<ip>`, `ask:ip:<ip>` and `ask:room:<room>`, and for a token it moves its expiry. When steps 2 and 6 found neither a session nor a token for a token-shaped cookie, the transaction first re-reads that cookie's hash in `sessions` and in `ask_sessions` in one statement over one snapshot (`select (select id from sessions where token_hash = $1 and expires_at > now() and purpose = 'room'), (select id from ask_sessions where token_hash = $1 and expires_at > now())`, the `purpose` test as `#find` has it, so a display token never counts as a room session), bypassing the request's memo (sessions.ts:103) and the negative cache, because a let-in may have promoted it since. One snapshot sees a promotion either wholly before or wholly after, since the promotion's insert and delete commit together, so the read can never find neither for a live token. A `sessions` row means the device is that session: the ask goes on with it, and final admission answers `409 already_in` once its let-in is done. An `ask_sessions` row means the token is live and is reused. Only with neither, which no later promotion can change, is a fresh token generated and its `ask_sessions` row inserted. So an ask never mints a replacement for a cookie whose hash has a session. A timeout or error answers `503 busy`. The locks live in the one server process (as room actors do), so a waiting ask holds no pooled connection, and the locked section makes no network call. They are released in a `finally`. (7) Every refusal or error after step 4 releases the reservation, in a `finally`. (8) Final admission, synchronous in the store, so no let-in, turn away, join or ending can slip between its checks and its write: the ask is within its 30 s deadline (else `503 busy`), the generation is unchanged (else `asks_off` or `room_not_found`), and the device's request here is not deciding (`409 request_deciding`), not let in, not marked as got in and holding no grant here whose member exists (`409 already_in`), and not held (`429 asked_recently`). A waiting one is replaced. The reservation becomes the request, the room cookie is set for a new token, and the DMs are signalled. A token made for an ask refused here is never handed out and expires within the hour.

**The wait.** The id, the path's room and the caller's session or token are all checked first, and a mismatch is `404 request_gone` before any held wait is touched or displaced. A held wait reads nothing from the database and holds no pool connection. It ends at 25 s, on the request's ending, when a newer wait displaces it (`superseded`), on `reply.raw` close (its timer and listener let go, as receipts.ts does), or at shutdown. Only an "in" answer then reads the grant and member.

**`admitPlayer`, with every step bounded.** (1) Cheap checks without a lock (the mode, a former DM, the session or token still live, no grant here whose member exists), so a member is rarely added for nothing. (2) For a new player, the member is marked in flight and `gateway.addMember` runs through the actor (`manager.acquire`, which loads the room, then the queued op, which the actor retries until it lands). The HTTP answer waits at most 5 s from the start of this step, the room's acquisition included. A `RoomFullError` wrote nothing and answers `409 room_full`. Past 5 s the attempt is still tracked, and a slow load is never taken for a failed member operation: the let-in's request stays `deciding` with the call kept in `pending` and the DM gets `202 {pending: true}`, while `join/complete` answers `503 busy` with its ticket reserved. The attempt goes on when the call lands, or settles when it fails. No grant exists meanwhile, so nobody is locked out. (3) The grant transaction, after a checkout bounded at 2 s (`withTx`'s new `checkoutMs`, which releases a connection that arrives late), with `set local lock_timeout = '2s'` and `statement_timeout = '5s'`: lock the room row with `join_mode`, `player_epoch` and the caller's checks (the let-in ends the request as closed in With the password, `join/complete` refuses `asks_only` and a stale ticket), recheck `already_in` and a carry-on member against `formerDms`, promote an ask token (5.1), and write the grant through `putGrant`. Accounting is the caller's: a let-in records its success and its let-in for the address's ask count, and `join/complete` records nothing more. (4) A failure before `commit` wrote no grant. `withTx` throws `CommitOutcomeUnknown`, moved from server/rooms/persistence.ts to server/auth/tx.ts, when `commit` itself fails. The caller then reconciles on a fresh bounded checkout: it locks the room row, which waits for the first transaction to end, and reads the session's grant in the room. A player grant naming this member at the attempt's `player_epoch`, or a DM grant naming it at the room's current `dm_epoch` (the device's own DM link may have upgraded it since, and DM grants carry the DM epoch), means committed. Anything else leaves the outcome unknown for good, since a grant that committed may have been kicked since: a let-in's request ends as gone (its own row in the table below), `join/complete`'s ticket goes back, and the member stays, an unused member the DM can remove, as after a crash. A reconciliation that cannot get the lock or read is followed up by the sweep, the let-in staying `deciding` and `join/complete`'s ticket staying reserved. (5) Only a confirmed rollback removes the member: a failure known to come before the grant could commit (a refused check, a statement error, a timeout or a checkout that never came, all before `commit` was sent). Then the undo follows Remove (server/http/members.ts): revoke every grant naming the member in the room, remove it through the actor (`removeMember`, under the same 5 s bound), revoke again. A step that does not land is owed and retried by the sweep. Either way the member then leaves flight, and `join/complete`'s ticket goes back once the attempt has settled, if its 5 minutes are not up. (6) After a commit, the member is checked once more. A DM who removed it meanwhile gets this exact grant revoked, matched on session, room, member id and epoch, as `join/complete` does today with its `member_removed` answer. Then the member leaves flight. The requester's wait answers "in" only once the grant is committed and its member is there.

**`join/complete` while its outcome is unknown.** The ticket is reserved for the attempt (`JoinTickets` gains a reserved state between `take` and `restore`): a retry with it answers `503 busy` again rather than `bad_ticket`, and nobody else can use it. When a late actor call lands, the attempt goes on to its grant in the background. Once it settles, a committed join is found by the page's `GET /me`, and a failed one gets its ticket back, so the retry joins. "Who are you?" treats `already_in` (another tab of the device finished first) as it treats `busy`: `GET /me`, then enter. A ticket whose 5 minutes run out first ends as today ("That took a while, so press Next again."). The comments at server/http/members.ts (Remove's second sweep) and server/http/join.ts (the old grant-then-member order) are rewritten to match.

**The mode switch** is one statement under the row lock, reading the current mode in the same statement:

```sql
with prev as (select join_mode, player_pw_hash_held from rooms where id = $1 for update)
update rooms r set
  join_mode = $2,
  player_pw_hash = case when prev.join_mode <> 'ask_only' and $2 = 'ask_only' then 'ask-only'
                        when prev.join_mode = 'ask_only' and $2 <> 'ask_only' then prev.player_pw_hash_held
                        else r.player_pw_hash end,
  player_pw_hash_held = case when prev.join_mode <> 'ask_only' and $2 = 'ask_only' then r.player_pw_hash
                             when prev.join_mode = 'ask_only' and $2 <> 'ask_only' then null
                             else r.player_pw_hash_held end
from prev
where r.id = $1 and prev.join_mode <> $2
  and not (prev.join_mode = 'ask_only' and $2 <> 'ask_only' and prev.player_pw_hash_held is null)
returning prev.join_mode
```

No row back is told apart by one read: no room is `404 room_not_found`, the mode already set answers `{mode}` with no bump and no signal, and a missing held hash is `409 no_password`. Hashes move only when the room enters or leaves Only by asking, so a repeated, retried or concurrent identical switch never copies the sentinel over the real hash. `ask-only` is not a PHC string, so `PasswordHasher.verify` on any build reads it as wrong. After it commits: entering Only by asking drops the room's tickets, a switch to With the password bumps the generation and ends the waiting requests as closed, and every real change signals the DMs. **A password change** is one statement too: `update rooms set player_pw_hash = case when join_mode = 'ask_only' then player_pw_hash else $2 end, player_pw_hash_held = case when join_mode = 'ask_only' then $2 else player_pw_hash_held end, player_epoch = player_epoch + 1 where id = $1`, with the sealed copy as today. db/schema.sql also runs, on every boot, `update rooms set player_pw_hash_held = player_pw_hash, player_pw_hash = 'ask-only' where join_mode = 'ask_only' and player_pw_hash <> 'ask-only'`, so a password an older build wrote is kept but closed again.

**Shutdown.** The routes' scope registers a `preClose` hook, as the socket plugin does (server/rooms/ws.ts): it stops taking waits (a new one answers "waiting" at once) and answers every held wait "waiting" at once, so `closeServer` never waits out a 25 s hold before the actors' final writes (main.ts exits at 25 s). The sweep's timer is cleared on close. **Room deletion**: `manager.onDelete` (server/rooms/manager.ts), wired in server/app.ts, drops the room's requests and bumps its generation as the delete starts, and `deleteRoomAndFiles` (server/http/rooms.ts), the one path both the DM's delete and the admin page's take, does both again after `gateway.deleteRoom`, through `app.joinRequests`, so an ask that slipped between the two finds the generation moved.

**The socket.** server/rooms/ws.ts is unchanged. An earlier draft had it refuse a grant whose member was missing, but with the member written first no new path makes such a grant, and the room route no longer opens a room without a member, so the refusal would only turn an old orphan into a reconnect loop.

New `ApiErrorCode`s in shared/apiErrors.ts: `asks_off`, `asks_only`, `asks_full`, `asked_recently`, `already_in`, `request_gone`, `request_answered`, `request_deciding`, `no_password`. `busy` and the Turnstile codes exist already.

### 5.3 The signal

shared/protocol.ts adds the server message `{t: "joinRequests"}`, sent by the actor's new `notifyJoinRequests()` to DM sockets that hold a snapshot and have no `previewBy`, through a new `RoomGateway.joinRequestsChanged(roomId)` (a no-op in DirectGateway and for a room not loaded). It goes out on every ask, answer, ending, sweep change and real mode change. It is apart from `access`, so a burst of asks never makes the DM devices list and the player link fetch again. The client counts it in `joinRequestsVersion`, which every snapshot also bumps, as `accessVersion` does, and fetches the list on each change. An older page drops the unknown frame, as it drops `clock`.

### 5.4 Versions, replay, migration, export, rollback

- **No OP_VERSION or SNAPSHOT_VERSION step**: they stay what this build's base has, 41 and 31. No op, no state field and no projection changes. A let-in reuses `sys.memberJoin` and, when its grant fails, `sys.memberRemove`, exactly. Server messages are not stored, so the new one replays nothing.
- **Migration**: two columns and one table. `join_mode` defaults to `password`, so every room keeps today's joins.
- **Export and import**: the mode and the held hash are server data like the password, never in a room's zip. An imported room starts With the password, as a new room does, and FEATURES.md and the DM guide say so. The nightly `pg_dump` keeps them.
- **Rollback**: an older build ignores the new columns and table. It checks passwords against `player_pw_hash`, which in an Only by asking room is the sentinel, so such a room lets nobody new in by password and nobody can ask there either. Devices already in stay in. The DM's password change on the older build opens password joins again, and coming forward closes them again at boot. An ask token is an unknown cookie to the older build, so it gains nothing there. A member added for a join that an older build's process never granted is an unused member the DM can remove. [self-hosting.md](../self-hosting.md)'s Updating section says this. Itests run the older join query and hash check against an Only by asking room, and the older session lookup against an ask token.

## 7. Performance and caps

A waiting phone makes one request every 25 seconds and renders only when its state changes. A DM device fetches one small list (at most 50 rows, each name at most 80 characters) per signal and renders on change. The server holds at most 500 records of about 300 bytes and at most 500 held waits, each a timer and an open response with no database connection, and a requester's calls count against its address's 120 a minute. An ask holds a pooled connection only for one short transaction of at most three statements, and a grant for one bounded transaction. Writing the member before the grant adds no step to a join, only a new order. No map, image or argon2 work is involved.

## 12. Decisions

- **D-261102-105** Three ways in, set in Members: With the password (the default, today's behavior), With the password, or by asking, and Only by asking, kept in the first release with the fail-closed boundary of 5.4 (the answer to the reviewers' first question in round 1). Rejected: one on and off switch (it loses Only by asking, the one mode where a kick keeps someone out and guessing is pointless), shipping Only by asking later (the boundary is two statements), and password plus approval for every device (two steps for every phone at the table, and the player link would be half a key). Why: the owner's rooms lean on the player link at the table, while a DM who wants control gets it in one tap.
- **D-261102-106** The mode and the held hash are `rooms` columns and requests live in server memory, so no op, no room state and no version step, and neither is exported. Rejected: a room setting through an op (it would reach players' devices and step both versions for access data), and a requests table (a stranger's typed name in the database, for a record that matters for 10 minutes). Why: the password, tickets and display codes already live this way.
- **D-261102-107** A requester learns nothing about the room before a let-in: no roster, no colors in use, no presence, and asks are taken whether or not a DM is online. Rejected: "The DM is not here right now" (tells anyone with the ID when the group plays), and marking colors in use (they hint who is in the room). Why: FEATURES.md's first promise, carried to people who are not yet members.
- **D-261102-108** (D-261102-009 and D-261102-011) A device asks with its own room session. One with none gets an ask token at admission, in the same short locked step, kept in `ask_sessions`, never in `sessions`: no user agent, an hour's life, no allowance. Whatever makes the device a session first (a let-in, a password join, a DM claim, a new room) promotes it into `sessions` with the same id and token under a lock on its row, and nothing is ever demoted. Rejected: no session until a let-in, with per-room ask cookies, collection and cookie swaps (six faults in that machinery over rounds 1 to 3), provisional rows in `sessions` (an older build would read them as full sessions, and demotion raced other rooms' grants, round 4), a full session at the ask (each one a 600 a minute allowance for 180 days), a token in page storage (script could read it), and a socket for requesters (design 10.4 admits only grants). Why: the let-in writes the grant onto the very session the device's cookie names, and strangers get only the per-address limits on this build and on an older one.
- **D-261102-109** Limits: per address 30 asks an hour per room (let-ins not counted) and 60 across rooms, 8 waiting per address per room, 50 per room and 500 on the server as memory bounds, Turnstile on every ask once a room passes 20 in an hour (rechecked under the admission locks) or from a held address without a real session from before the hold, one request per device per room, and 15 minutes before a turned-away device may ask that room again. There is no hourly refusal per room, and the way out of a crowded or challenged room is switching to With the password, or by asking. Rejected: a per-room hourly refusal (lets a stranger close a room's asks, against decision 9), Turnstile on every ask (most asks come from a table that should see nothing), a per-address hold after a turn away (it would hold back a whole table on one home connection), and Postgres advisory locks for admission (a waiting ask would park one of the pool's 10 connections). Why: sized for a table of about 8 phones, as wave 5's per-member limits are.
- **D-261102-110** A request lasts its 10 minutes whatever its page does, and keeps its outcome 2 minutes after. A let-in whose outcome is unknown stays `deciding` until it is known, reconciled against its exact grant under the room lock. Rejected: ending a request whose page stops waiting (a locked iPhone or a suspended Safari tab loses its place), ending it on `pagehide` (fires in ways that would cancel a phone that only switched apps), a fixed deadline that restores `waiting` (the actor may still land the member), and treating any grant as proof of commit (a password join may have replaced it). Why: the same 10 minutes as invites and display codes, and no request ever claims a state the database contradicts.
- **D-261102-111** "Someone new" is the default. Carrying on as a listed player (never a former DM) is a separate pick, and every such pick names what it hands over and whether that player is online, with a matching name only a hint (the answer to the reviewers' second question in round 1: keep "Let in as"). Rejected: warning only on a matching name (look-alike letters evade it), matching the name on its own (a stranger who types a player's name would get their whispers from a quick tap), and no carry-on (a player on a new phone without the password could not stay themselves). Why: the DM's deliberate, informed choice carries the same trust the password does.
- **D-261102-112** The DM hears by a toast with Review, the DM panel's badge, and a Join requests section at the top of the DM panel while any wait. The mode lives in Members. Rejected: a card in the fight's alerts column (its layout rules are built for fights, and asks come mostly before one), and Let in and Turn away inside the toast (toasts carry one action, and a stray tap would let a stranger in). Why: the DM panel already holds the player link "for a latecomer", and the upload badge shows the pattern.
- **D-261102-113** Any DM device answers, co-DMs included, the first answer wins, and who answered goes only to the server log by member id. Rejected: a DM-only log line "Mara let Quinn in" (a new event field and an OP_VERSION step for little), and only the room's creator answering (co-DMs hold full DM rights everywhere else). Why: one rule for every DM, as kicks and password changes.
- **D-261102-114** Logging: the room log keeps only today's join line for a new member, and a removal line if its grant fails, while the server log keeps ids and outcomes and never a name, color, token, session or address. Rejected: logging the typed name (a stranger's words in the operator's logs), and a DM-visible list of past refusals (more stored data for little use). Why: the X-card and the player link already keep personal words out of the logs.

Eight rounds, each with a Claude and a Codex reviewer, every finding adopted. Rounds 1 and 2 replaced a per-room refusal with a Turnstile threshold and quieter toasts, had `join/complete` refuse Only by asking under its lock, added the password boundary, `preClose`, `superseded`, numbered DM fetches and the 10-minute life, and bounded the let-in end to end. ## As built

- **`mine` answers 204.** `GET /api/rooms/:id/join-requests/mine` answers `204` with no body for a device that holds no request, where 5.2 said `404 request_gone`. Most devices that open the join page in the asking modes hold none, and Chrome logged every such 404 as an error. `api.getMyJoinRequest` returns null for it.
- **Cancel cuts off its own held wait** before it is sent, and no wait runs until Cancel answers, so the wait's `404 request_gone` never reaches the page or its console, and Cancel's answer alone decides what comes next: the form, waiting on, or `GET /me`.
- **A kicked player's device** that opens the room within 2 minutes of its let-in finds the kept `in` ending, sees no grant and shows "Request ended" with Ask again, then the ask card.
- **`mine` reports a deciding request as `waiting`**, so the join page has no state of its own for it. A wait's `in` answer is `404 request_gone` if the grant was kicked before it was read. Mode changes send only the `joinRequests` signal, never `access`.
- `CommitOutcomeUnknown` lives in server/auth/tx.ts, and server/rooms/persistence.ts re-exports it, so actor.ts keeps its import. actor.ts gains only `notifyJoinRequests`.
- Members in flight and owed removals live in `Admissions` (server/auth/admit.ts), not the store, and the sweep runs `Admissions.followUp`. A member whose removal is owed stays in flight until the removal lands, where 5.2 had it leave flight either way.
- The 5 s bound is the answer's, from the attempt's start, in every phase: the member, the grant, the read-back after a lost `COMMIT` and giving a grant up. Past it the let-in answers `202` and `join/complete` `503 busy`, and the attempt settles on its own. A read-back that cannot connect answers that at once rather than waiting on the sweep. The grant transaction keeps its own bounds: a 2 s checkout, a 2 s lock timeout and a 5 s statement timeout.
- **A refusal is answered as soon as it is known.** The undo that takes the new member back off runs behind it, with every revoke bounded (2 s checkout, 2 s lock and statement timeouts) and the member kept in flight until it lands, so a stalled pool costs `503 busy` at the checkout's 2 s. A revoke past its bounds is owed and retried by the sweep, and so is the exact revoke after a commit whose member was removed. Reading that member back is bounded at 5 s.
- `join/complete` answers `409 already_in` for a session already in the room, instead of replacing its grant.
- A refused let-in whose request settled into an ending answers `404 request_gone`, and so does one whose outcome stays unknown.
- The turn-away hold is read in the same `asks()` read as the counts, and holds are read back from `rate_events`, so a restart lifts none.
- `SessionStore.create`'s adoption of an ask token passes the device's own user agent. Only a let-in promotes with none.
- `JOIN_REQUEST_LIFE_S` in server/config.ts (5 to 600 s, ignored in production) shortens a request's life for the end-to-end expiry step.
- The crash between the member and the grant is shown by reading the database in the middle of an attempt, not by killing a process.
- With no `joinMode` from the server (an older server), the join page behaves as With the password.
- The ask card's check notice reads "Please confirm you're human before asking.".
- Back on an outcome card in Only by asking returns to the ask card. The waiting card's "Waiting for the DM…" line shows at once, with no 200 ms delay.
- The case table's RoomRoute unit is `hasMember` in client/pages/askToJoin.test.ts, not a rendered route.
- With two or more waiting when a DM device opens the room, its toast reads "Quinn and 2 more are waiting to join.".
- **A request being let in stays on the DM list**, marked `deciding`, and `pending` once its let-in answered `202`, so the tapping device's row keeps its busy words and any refusal through the list's fetches, and a co-DM's row is busy too. A request whose let-in fails waits again with the same id, so no device toasts it twice. `not_a_player` uses the `member_removed` words.
- **Kicks against admissions**: join/complete and the let-in take a number from `Admissions.begin()` before they read the member they name, and Kick and Remove mark the member before they revoke. An admission begun before a kick of its member refuses at its grant's check with `409 member_removed`, and its new member comes off as after any rollback. The kick's revoke locks the room row first, which an admission past that check holds until its grant commits, so the revoke deletes that grant before the kick answers. A kick that gets no connection or lock within 2 s answers `503 busy`, not done. A route has 60 s from `begin()` to its grant's check, its hash and a cold room's member step included, and one past it writes no grant and answers `503 busy`. A kick is remembered for 2 minutes, twice that, so no route that began before a kick can still grant once the kick is forgotten.
- **Relabels after a revoke are bounded** at 5 s in a join's undo and in Kick and Remove. One that the room's actor does not take in time leaves the kick done and the label owed, and the sweep sets it back unless the member holds a DM grant again. The sweep's follow-ups run room by room, so a room whose actor or rows stall holds up no other room's.
- **The carry-on list hears its changes**: a new member leaving flight, and any device getting in, signal the DM list, so "Let in as" offers a password joiner as soon as its grant lands rather than at the next unrelated fetch.
- **A failed let-in says why on every DM device.** The DM list carries `refused`, the last let-in's refusal code, for 60 s while the request waits, so a let-in that fails after its `202`, or on another DM device, shows its words on every row.
