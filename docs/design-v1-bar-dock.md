# raccoon-vtt v1: the docked token action bar

Status: built. Draft 4 with the round 4 fixes reached consensus at design review round 4, and the build has landed: `client/canvas/barDock.ts` holds the layout rules, and the code review's round 1 fixes are in. Code is cited from main at `ee3d0b82`, which holds the height readout build, so line numbers below are from before the build. Section 3 builds on that build's hold signal and on its `client/canvas/emptyTap.ts`.

The project owner asked on 2026-10-15: "when select several is being used, the pane used for damage, conditions, etc should be positioned so it does not overlap any creatures to make them easier to select." They chose: on large screens the bar docks at the bottom right of the map, drags by its header, remembers its spot on each device and can be put back, and Fit and Find keep tokens out from under it. The single-token bar works the same way as the several-tokens bar, so there is one place to look. Phones held upright keep their spanning bar. Then: "also clicking off of the pane that appears should make it go away, just make sure it does not interfere dragging around to see the map on mobile". After round 1 they settled Select several: "tapping off makes the pane itself go away, but does not deselect the group", and "i would also like tapping a selected token in a group to deselect it".

The fight alerts column and the floating notices share the right side of the map, so this design also settles five live play findings: LPD-PLAYER-2 and LPD-DMPHONE-3 (the column covers the bar), LPD-DMPHONE-4 (the column's margin runs past the screen), and LPD-DMPHONE-9 and LPD-DESK-6 (a notice covers the column's first card).

Every size is in layout pixels, the chrome's own (`features/device/layout.ts`), so the interface size changes them as it changes everything else. A safe area inset counts at its full size in layout pixels too, since the chrome adds `env()` inside its zoom: the toolbar's height is `calc(safe.bottom + 60px)` (`Room.tsx:904`, zoomed at `:909`), the top bar pads by `safe.top`, and the column's `right` holds `safe.right` (`InitiativeStrip.tsx:435`). So at 130% a 47 px notch takes 47 layout px. The bar takes the measured insets (Room's `insets`, whose rectangles already hold those zoomed insets) and the same full numbers at the sides, so the lane lines up with the column.

## 1. Where the bar sits

### 1.1 Span, dock or compact

`barMode` in a new `client/canvas/barDock.ts` gives one of three:

- **Upright span**: a layout under 600 px wide and no wider than tall, whose free area (1.2) is at least 148 px tall. As today, the bar spans the screen with `space(2)` margins, hangs under or over its token, and folds into the dock under a sheet (docs/design-v1-visual.md 3.2.1). One thing changes: the selected token's plate now shows (section 6), so `belowLabel` (`Stage.tsx:1093-1097`) hangs the bar lower under a token with a plate, and it flips above the token more often. It never moves beside the token in portrait (`TokenActionBar.tsx:521`).
- **Dock**: a layout 600 px or wider, or wider than tall, whose free area is at least 148 px tall. 148 is the header (48) and two 44 px button rows with their gaps, or under the alerts summary row (1.3) the header and one row. That covers every layout 600 px or wider and phones held sideways (the project owner's choice, since LPD-PLAYER-2 happened there).
- **Compact**: any layout, upright or not, whose free area is under 148 px tall, down to 0 or less. Section 1.4.

600 lies above every phone held upright at 100% (the widest is 440) and at or below most tablets at 100% (600 for the smallest Android tablets, 744 for an iPad mini). A larger interface size can bring an upright tablet under it (an iPad at 768 px and 130% lays out at 591), and then it spans as a phone does. The fold rule (`foldsUnderSheet`) does not change.

### 1.2 The free area and the default spot

A docked bar lives in the **free area**. That is the map area (which from 900 px up already ends at the side panel), less the measured top inset (top bar, turn band and safe area, Room's `insets.top`), the measured bottom inset (toolbar, the active tool's options such as the Select several bar, and safe area, `insets.bottom`), the side safe areas and a phone's landscape sheet, and an 8 px margin (`space(2)`) all round. Stage passes Room's `insets` to the bar, so `BOTTOM_RESERVE`, `reserveBottom` and `SELECT_OPTIONS_PX` go.

The default spot is the free area's bottom right corner. The bar is `min(304, free width)` wide and grows upward, as tall as its content up to the room it has. "Scroll for more" stays. When docked or spanning, the header is `position: sticky` at the top of the scrolling frame, so the handle and Close never scroll away. It has an opaque `surface` background that reaches across the frame's 12 px side padding, and it clips the name to two lines. The bar still hides during a token drag, so the whole map shows while a token moves.

### 1.3 The right lane: the column, the bar and the notices

The **lane** is the right 312 px of the free area: room for the 304 px bar (the column is 300) and an 8 px gap before the map. The alerts column hangs at its top. The docked bar sits at its bottom by default.

- **Who gives way.** `shareLane` settles a bar and a column with cards that overlap across (a bar in the lane, or any spanning bar). Its inputs are the lane's height `L` (the free height when docked, the overlay's when compact), the column's first card `F`, and the bar's floor `f = min(120, L)`. It takes the first of these that fits:
  1. **Stacked.** If `L − F − 8 ≥ f`, the column keeps at least its first card, and the bar sits below it with an 8 px gap: its top is `max(wanted top, column top + F + 8)`, where the wanted top comes from the bar's spot (for an upright span, its place by its token). The column's max height stops 8 px above the bar. Beyond its first card the column gives way first, then the bar down to `f`.
  2. **Beside.** Else, if the free width less the lane is at least 230 px (the narrowest bar the app already draws: an upright span at 320 px and 130%), the bar moves left of the lane, `min(304, that room)` wide and the lane's full height. The column keeps the lane whole. The stored spot is left alone.
  3. **Summary.** Else the column shows a 44 px alerts summary row in its place, and the bar takes the rest of the lane below it, 8 px down.

  With no cards in the column there is no gap and the bar takes the whole lane. Every output is clamped at 0, so `L` of 0 or less gives a defined, empty result.
- **The summary row** is the dock's alerts summary (`alertsSummary` in `pages/fold.ts`, drawn by RoomDock's summary button). Its advice becomes a parameter that covers all three of today's wordings (`fold.ts:116`): "Hide the bar to answer." when a roll waits for this viewer, else "Hide the bar to see them." or "Hide the bar to see it.". The column's cards take `hidden` meanwhile, as in the fold, and `FoldStatus` speaks each alert that arrives. Its tap hides the bar (`showActions(false)`) and shows the column. Then, as the dock's does, it opens a roll that waits for this viewer and focuses it, or focuses the first alert. The bar comes back with a token tap, or with Actions in Select several.
- **A short screen's summary row.** The column stops at the active tool's options. Where the room down to them is under the column's first whole card (measured), the same row stands for the cards, uncapped, with "Tap to answer.", "Tap to see them." or "Tap to see it.". Its bottom keeps above the options' highest button or field (`Chrome.controls`), so it may cover a hint but never Actions or Keep. Where its place under the top bars and any lasting notice would put it over those buttons, as in a fight at 480 x 320 at 130%, it rises to the lowest spot above them that meets no lasting notice, over the turn band and then the top bar's row if it must (`raisedRow` in `canvas/barDock.ts`). A lasting notice paints above the column, so the row never stands under one. Over a button in the top bars it narrows from the right to stop 8 px left of it, End turn above all, keeping 44 px, or else rises further. It may cover the band's name or Next disks, or the room's name. Its tap opens a roll waiting for this viewer at a phone's foot. Otherwise, where the first card fits whole from the column's place down to the toolbar, it shows the cards there, over the tool's options, until a tap lands outside them or what waits changes. Else it opens the alerts over the screen where the column has no room (`askedAlerts`): a modal dialog, "Alerts", from the safe top to the toolbar, or to the screen's foot where even that is too short for the first card, on an opaque fill over the notices, with a 44 px Close in its header and the column's own cards in a scrolling body, so answering a check or a roll there works as in the column. Focus goes to the first card. Close, Escape and Back close it, as does nothing left waiting, and focus goes back to the row. While it is open the turn band, End turn included, sits under it, and Close is one tap away.
- **A clipped column says so.** While the stacked column is cut short, it shows the bar's sticky `aria-hidden` "Scroll for more" fade at its foot.
- **Measuring.** The column publishes its content height and its first card's height to a slot in the Room scope. The first card is the first child with a box, looking inside the rolls' `display: contents` wrapper (`InitiativeStrip.tsx:455`). The bar publishes its drawn box. The bar reads only the column's content heights and the column only the bar's box, so neither feeds back on itself.
- **Applied on release.** During a drag the bar follows the finger, clamped only to the free area, and may pass under the column (z 15 over 5). The lane rule moves it on release, and after Move bar.
- **Notices.** The lasting ones are the offline banner (`Banner`), a map layer that did not load (`QuietLoadNotice`), the DM's frame bar (`FrameViewBar`) and "Viewing as Pat". Toasts (whispers, refusals) last 5 s. When the free width less the lane is at least 320 px, the whole notice stack, toasts included, centers in the free area left of the lane, column or not, so nothing moves when an alert arrives. Otherwise the stack spans as today, and the column's top moves 8 px below the last lasting notice. Toasts keep today's place there, so a card never jumps under a finger for a toast. The scene loading notice sits centered under the top bar's layer (z 5 under 15), so it never covers the column.
- **Viewing as Pat** stays in PlayerPreview, outside the previewed Room, with its Exit and its `flash` state, so a blackout and the ended screen keep Exit. Through `PreviewContext`, PlayerPreview passes in the notice's measured height, and Room reports back the box the notice takes by the rule above, in window pixels. Beside the lane it centers left of it. On a narrow screen it spans as today, and Room's own notices and the column start below it (today Room's notices sit under it). While Room reports nothing (a blackout, the ended screen, the first frame), it sits where it does today.
- **Inside the screen** (LPD-DMPHONE-4). Nothing in the lane reaches past the free area's right edge. The column's scroll box ends at the screen edge, or at a landscape sheet's left edge, and its right padding is the 8 px margin rather than the 20 px shadow room. A card scrolled into view then never scrolls the room frame.

### 1.4 Compact

On a screen too short to dock or span, the bar opens as an overlay.

- **Across**, it takes the dock's place (the free area's right `min(304, free width)`, or left of a landscape sheet), or for an upright layout the span's full width.
- **Down**, it runs from 8 px under the top inset to 8 px above the toolbar when that leaves it 148 px, so it covers only the Select several bar. Otherwise it runs to 8 px above the bottom safe area and covers the toolbar too.
- **Above** the toolbar and top bar (`z.bars`, 15) and the options bar (`z.options`, 14), and below notices (`z.notices`, 20), at a new `theme.z.barOverlay` of 16.
- `shareLane` places it against the column with its height as `L`. It scrolls as a whole, and its header is not sticky, so all of that height serves the controls. It does not move: no handle, Move bar or Reset bar position, and the stored spot is left alone. Its box counts for Fit and Find like a docked bar's. Under a portrait sheet it folds, as the bar does today.
- Covering the Select several bar hides Done and Actions, which loses nothing: the bar keeps its own Select several toggle and its close. Covering the toolbar on a phone held sideways also hides its right end, Panels and its unread badge, until the bar closes. An upright overlay spans the full width, so it covers every tool button as well as Panels until Close.

The numbers, with Select several on (its bar one 52 px row, section 3) and the insets at full size:

- **480 x 320 at 130%**, 47 px at the sides and 21 at the bottom, lays out at 369 x 246. Free width 369 − 47 − 47 − 16 = 259. Free height 246 − 52 (top bar) − 81 (toolbar and home bar) − 52 − 16 = 45, or −3 with a 48 px turn band: compact. Down to the toolbar leaves 97 (49 with the band), under 148, so the overlay covers the toolbar too: 246 − 52 − 21 − 16 = 157 tall, or 109 with the band. 259 px leaves no room beside a lane, so an alert shows as the summary row, and the bar keeps 105, or 57 with the band.
- **844 x 390 at 130%**, the same insets, lays out at 649 x 300. Free width 649 − 94 − 16 = 539. Free height 300 − 52 − 81 − 52 − 16 = 99, or 51 with the band: compact. Down to the toolbar leaves 151, so the overlay covers only the Select several bar and is 151 tall. With the band that leaves 103, so it covers the toolbar too: 300 − 52 − 48 − 21 − 16 = 163. 539 − 312 = 227 is under 230, so an alert shows as the summary row, and the bar keeps 99, or 111 with the band. Without Select several the free height is 151, so the bar docks, and an alert's summary row leaves it 99: the header and a row.
- **320 x 480 at 130%**, upright, 44 px at the top and 34 at the bottom, lays out at 246 x 369. Free height 369 − 96 (top bar and notch) − 48 (band) − 94 (toolbar and home bar) − 52 − 16 = 63: compact, 230 wide. Down to the toolbar leaves 115, so it covers the toolbar too: 369 − 96 − 48 − 34 − 16 = 175 tall, and an alert's summary row leaves the bar 123. Without the band the free height is 111, still compact. Down to the toolbar then leaves 163, so it covers only the Select several bar, and the summary row leaves the bar 111.

## 2. Moving the bar

- **The handle** is the header row. A grip icon at its start shows that it moves (`aria-hidden`). A primary press on the header, outside its buttons, takes `setPointerCapture`. The header has `touch-action: none` and, for a mouse, the `grab` and `grabbing` cursors. The bar moves once the pointer passes `TAP_SLOP_PX` (8 px), so a tap on the header does nothing. Touch, pen and mouse take the same path.
- **Kept clear.** During the drag the box is clamped into the free area, so it never covers the top bar, the turn band, the toolbar, the options bar, the side panel or a landscape sheet. On release the lane rule applies.
- **Snapping.** On release, any edge within 24 px of the free area's edge snaps to it. Dropped near the bottom right corner, the bar is in its default spot again. There is no grid.
- **Escape or `pointercancel` during a drag** puts the bar back where the drag began and keeps it open. A press on the header focuses the frame, so a listener on the header would never see the key. Instead a capture-phase keydown listener on window, added when the drag starts and removed when it ends, takes Escape and calls `preventDefault`. Room's `useEscape`, a bubble listener that skips handled keys, then leaves the bar alone.
- **Stored per device.** Release writes localStorage key `rvtt.tokenBarSpot`, read and written in try and catch as every `rvtt.*` key is, for example `{"corner":"tl","x":120,"y":40}`. `corner` is the free area's corner nearest the bar's center (`tl`, `tr`, `bl` or `br`). `x` and `y` are the gaps from that corner's two edges to the bar's matching corner, integers from 0 to 4096. Anything else reads as the default, and the default is stored as no key. One spot serves every room and both roles. A DM's "view as a player" reads it and keeps a drag to itself, since nothing done there is saved.
- **Changes of size.** The box is worked out again from the corner and gaps whenever the free area changes: a resize, a rotation, a new interface size, the turn band coming or going, the options bar wrapping, a landscape sheet opening or closing. The gaps are clamped to keep the bar inside, and the stored value is left alone, so the bar returns to its spot when the room comes back. A bar in a right corner follows the free area's right edge, past a landscape sheet and back. The side panel from 900 px up never opens or closes: crossing 900 px is a resize.
- **Move bar.** A button whose name is its text, "Move bar", in the bar's button row while it docks. Each press sends the bar to the next corner of the free area, clockwise from the bottom right, flush with it, and stores it. A visually hidden polite status in the bar then says where it went: "Bar at bottom left", "Bar at top left", "Bar at top right", "Bar at bottom right". It is the single-pointer and keyboard way to move the bar (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.7), and focus stays on it.
- **Reset bar position.** A button whose name is its text, after Move bar, shown only while the bar is away from its default spot. It removes the key. Focus moves to Move bar, since this button goes away.
- There is no arrow-key moving, since the arrow keys nudge the selected tokens.

## 3. Taps off the bar

On main (`ee3d0b82`), a tap on empty map reaches `CameraGesture.up` (`lib/gestures.ts:99-108`), which reports `tapOnEmpty` and `held`, and Stage runs `emptyTap(ctx, tool, world, held, deselect)` (`Stage.tsx:757`, `emptyTap.ts:29-39`). With Select several on, a press on empty map is a box gesture instead: Stage passes `onEmpty = false` (`Stage.tsx:658-661`), so `tapOnEmpty` never fires, and the box's release (`:748-751`) acts only past `TAP_SLOP_PX`.

- **Select several off** (one token, or a Shift group): a tap on empty map lets go and closes the bar, as today, and `emptyTap` goes on to a label, a door or the height readout.
- **Select several on**: a tap off hides the bar and keeps the group, its outlines and the mode. Stage's box release counts a box as that tap when it moved no more than `TAP_SLOP_PX`, was not cancelled or held, and was not made with Shift. It calls `onSelect(null)`, and `tapToken(local, null)` while selecting several now hides the bar (`showActions(false)`) instead of doing nothing. The box tap then puts floaters away as `emptyTap` does, and opens nothing. It closes a pin's popover unless a pin is under the tap (`emptyTap.ts:33`), so it never closes a popover that same tap opened, and it closes the height readout, which a selected token already keeps shut (`ReadoutHost.tsx:57`). Both paths call one helper taken out of `emptyTap`. A box tap opens no door or label, as today. A real box drag keeps adding to the group.
- **A token tap** with Select several off selects that token, and the bar shows it in the same spot. With Select several on, a tap toggles, as today: a token outside the group joins it and shows the bar again, and a token in it leaves the group without reopening a hidden bar (today any toggle reopens it). Taking out the last token empties the selection and closes the bar, and the mode stays on. A box drag that adds tokens shows the bar again too.
- **Actions.** A button whose name is its text, in the Select several bar, shown while the group has tokens and its bar is hidden. It shows the bar (`showActions(true)`) and focuses the bar's frame. While a landscape sheet or the panel list is open, Room draws no options bar (`Room.tsx:362`), so Actions is not there: the way back is to close the sheet, then press Actions, or to tap a token to add it.
- **The Select several bar stays one row.** Actions shows only while the bar is hidden, so it never adds height under an open bar. When the room the options bar may take is under 600 px inside its padding, where the hint's sentence (about 430 px at 13 px) beside Actions and Done (about 170 px with gaps) would wrap, the visible hint is just "3 selected.". The hint never decides from the bar's own drawn width. From 900 px up the options bar has `left` and `maxWidth: 640` but no `right` (`Room.tsx:846-856`), so it sizes to its content, and a width measured from it would lock in whichever hint it showed first. So on wide screens the room is `min(640, map width − 16)` less its padding and safe insets. On the phone layout the bar spans (`left: 0; right: 0`), so there it measures its own inner width with a ResizeObserver, after its safe-area padding (`Room.tsx:856`), not from the layout width: at 844 x 390 and 130% with 47 px notches the layout is 649 wide but the row only 649 − 2 × (47 + 8) = 539. The whole sentence becomes the toolbar's description (`aria-describedby` on "Select options").
- **Done** in the Select several bar turns the mode off and lets go of the group, closing its bar, as the project owner described it. Today it only turns the mode off and keeps the group and its bar (`setMultiSelect` in `state/selection.ts`, whose test says "Turning the mode off keeps the group, ready to move"). `setMultiSelect(false)` itself stays as it is. A new `doneSelecting` calls it and then lets go, and Done uses that. The bar's own "Select several" toggle keeps calling `setMultiSelect`, for someone who wants to leave the mode and still act on the group.
- **Escape and Back.** With the bar showing they do what the bar's close does, as today: they let go. With Select several on, a group held and its bar hidden, they do what Done does (`doneSelecting`), so Back never leaves the room while a group is held. Today both stop listening once the bar is hidden: Escape checks `local.actionBar` (`RoomChrome.tsx:115`) and Back registers only while it holds (`Room.tsx:208`). One function in `state/selection.ts`, `dismissal(local, tokenPanelShown)`, gives "bar", "group" or nothing, and both read it. It gives "group" only while the token panel is not showing the selection. That also covers a DM whose side panel happens to sit on the Token tab: with it open, Escape leaves the group alone, and Done is the way out. Details hides the bar and opens the token panel (`Room.tsx:163-169`), and from 900 px up that panel is the side panel's token tab, so Escape there leaves the panel and the group alone, as today. On a phone the token panel is a sheet, which Escape and Back close first anyway. The guard is on what shows rather than a flag the tap off sets, so the keys still work after the alerts summary hid the bar. A sheet, a dialog and a text field keep their precedence (`lib/escape.ts`, `lib/backClose.ts`).
- **Off** is whatever reaches the map canvas: empty map, the gaps between alert cards (the column passes taps through), and a map label or door drawn into the map. Everything else acts and leaves the selection alone: the bar, a sheet, the side panel, the toolbar and the Select several bar, the top bar, an alert card, the phone's dock, a notice, and panes on the map such as a door card or a stop note. These are DOM above the canvas, so their taps never reach it.
- **Not a tap:** a move past 8 px (a pan or a box drag), a second finger (a pinch), a mouse pan with the right or middle button or Space, the wheel, and a press the hold-to-ping fired for. "Held" is the readout build's signal, not a second clock: the ping layer reports each press it pinged (`features/pings/hold.ts`), `CameraGesture.hold(id)` marks it (`gestures.ts:89-90`), and `up` reports `held`. This design changes two things in that build. `up` reports `held` for any still one-finger press, not only one on empty map (`gestures.ts:103` drops `tapOnEmpty &&`), so the box path can read it. `emptyTap` returns before `deselect` for a held press (`emptyTap.ts:30-31` swap), so a ping neither deselects, hides the bar nor opens anything. The readout design lets a held press deselect.
- The rules are the same on every device. With Select several off a phone behaves as today, except that a hold to ping no longer lets go.

## 4. Fit, Find and pans

While a docked or compact bar shows, it publishes its drawn box to the Room scope. A folded, spanning or hidden bar publishes nothing: a spanning bar is placed by its token, not by the map. `barCover(viewport, bars, box)` turns the box into one inset. A bar in the lane covers the lane's side, the right 312 px plus the margin and safe area, whatever its height. A bar elsewhere covers the side that leaves the largest clear rectangle.

- **Fit the map** fits into the bars plus that cover, the larger on each side. The fit remembers the cover it used, so the bar opening or closing later never moves the fitted map, and a bar that grows still refits as today.
- **Find my token** and the outliner's show on map pass it to `findCamera` with the open sheet's cover, the larger on each side. The zoom ignores it, as it ignores the sheet, and the token lands centered in what is left.
- **Pans that bring a token into view** (a tracker row, the party bar, follow the turn) place their anchor in the area the cover leaves. A pan that opens the bar uses the bar's spot and the height it last had, since its box does not exist yet, shared with the column by the same placement the drawn bar takes (`placeBar` and `shareLane`), so a bar that will sit beside the lane is expected there. Without this, a 900 px window, whose map is 540 px wide, centers the token under the bar.
- Panes anchored to a map point (door card, stop note, label, light and pin panes) keep their anchors and draw over the bar, later in the same layer, as today.

## 5. What stays

Nothing loses a function, name or role. Both bars keep `role="dialog"` and their names ("Thorn actions", "3 selected tokens"), their closes ("Close", "Let go of all of them"), the Conditions drawer, More and its focus return, the mixed states (`aria-pressed="mixed"` and the dashed edge), the HP entry and the group HP entry, the busy and refused states (Undo move waiting on the server, Doesn't count refusing a second press, the armed Delete), the bar's own "Select several" toggle, the `key={selected.id}` remount, and "Scroll for more". The frame's script focus stays: `tabIndex={-1}`, focused by the dock's Show, by a closing sheet and now by Actions, never by a plain tap, so no keyboard opens. On phones the dock and the fold stay: `usePublishBar`, `useSheetReturnFocus` at `RETURN_RANK.tokenBar` (2), and `hidden` while folded with its state kept. The single and group bars share `BarFrame`, so both dock and move, and they share one spot.

## 6. Which token it acts on

- The header names it: the token's whole name, clipped to two lines, or "3 selected" with the names under it. The frame's name always holds the whole name.
- The token keeps its accent selection outline, and each token of a group keeps its own.
- The selected token's plate (its whole name on the map) now shows in every mode. It stepped aside only because the bar's header showed the whole name (`Stage.tsx:369-370`), which a long name no longer gets. Under an upright span this hangs the bar lower (1.1).

No line joins the bar to its token: it would cross the very tokens this change keeps clear.
